Email DNS Records Explained

SPF (Sender Policy Framework)

A TXT record listing authorized mail servers. Prevents email spoofing. Example: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all

DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)

Adds a digital signature to outgoing emails. Receiving servers verify the signature against the domain's public key in DNS. Selectors like google._domainkey.example.com or selector1._domainkey.example.com.

DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication)

A TXT record at _dmarc.example.com that tells receiving servers how to handle emails that fail SPF/DKIM. Example: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@example.com

MX (Mail Exchange)

Directs incoming email to the correct mail servers. Essential for receiving email. Example: aspmx.l.google.com with priority 1.
